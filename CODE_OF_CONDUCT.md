# Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of this repository, we are committed to fostering an open and welcoming environment. We are committed to respecting everyone who engages with this project, regardless of status, gender, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, or immigration status.

## Our Standards

We follow these principles in spirit as well as letter:

### Be considerate.

Differences of opinion do not have to lead to conflict. Disagree and be willing to listen.

### Be respectful.

We won't diminish others' contributions. Harassment includes but is not limited to:

- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Sustained disruption of discussion
- Inappropriate use of power (including deliberate exclusion from roles)

### Be careful.

Tread lightly when contacting a contributor about their contributions. Please review before issuing complaints.

## Reporting Harassment

If you feel you have been subjected to behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it by opening an issue on GitHub describing the incident. Maintainers will respond promptly.
